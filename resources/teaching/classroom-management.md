## The "Device Light" System
To prevent technology from interrupting direct instruction, use a visual status indicator on your whiteboard:

* **🔴 Red Light (Devices Closed):** Laptops closed, tablets facedown, phones away. Focus is entirely on the teacher or paper-based materials.
* **🟡 Yellow Light (Guided Devices):** Laptops open but active only on specified URLs (e.g., Google Docs, Canvas, or Quizlet). No side-browsing.
* **🟢 Green Light (Open Research/Collaboration):** Free device use. Students can research, write, collaborate, and use approved AI tools as directed.

## Managing Screen Distractions
1. **Strategic Seating Layouts:** Arrange desks in a U-shape or clusters that allow you to easily walk behind screens and see student workspaces.
2. **Tab Audits:** Gamify digital focus. Run "3-Tab Checks" where students receive participation points if they have only three relevant browser tabs open during study periods.
3. **Active Circulation:** Never sit at your desk while students are on laptops. Walk the room, asking questions and scanning screens to redirect off-task students.

## Establishing AI Usage Policies
To foster academic integrity, implement a clear "AI Degrees of Freedom" scale on every writing assignment sheet:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              AI USAGE LEVEL FOR THIS ASSIGNMENT           │
├──────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 0: 🚫   │ No AI use permitted. Human drafting only.  │
├──────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 1: ✏️   │ Edit-only. Spellcheck & grammar tools only.│
├──────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 2: 💡   │ Brainstorming helper. Outline support only.│
├──────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Level 3: 🤝   │ Full Co-Pilot. Writing partner mode.       │
└──────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Digital Warm-ups and Transitions
* **The "Zero-Tab" Welcome:** Have students start class with their laptops closed. Do a 5-minute verbal discussion or physical writing warm-up before screens open.
* **Timer Transitions:** Display visual timers (e.g., classroomscreen.com) when transitioning between digital work and partner talk.

## Teacher Cheat Sheet: Tech Routines

| Routine Name | What Students Do | Teacher Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screens Down, Eyes Up** | Students lower laptops to 45 degrees ("clamshell mode") within 5 seconds. | Give clear verbal countdown. |
| **Docking Station** | Phones are placed in designated pocket hangers upon entering the room. | Verify slot completion against attendance. |
| **Peer Logoff** | Partners verify that each other has saved work and signed out of accounts before packing up. | Set transition timer. |
