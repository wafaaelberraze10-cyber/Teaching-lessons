## Overview
Gemini is Google's natively multimodal AI model. Designed to process text, code, audio, image, and video data seamlessly, Gemini integrates directly into Google Workspace for Education (Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Classroom), making it a powerful utility for educators who run their classrooms using Google tools.

---

## Use Cases in the English Classroom
* **Multimodal Literacy Lessons:** Generating activities that require students to analyze videos, image art, and written text side-by-side.
* **Workspace Integration:** Drafting lesson plans, emails, and student rubrics directly inside Google Docs and Gmail using Gemini extensions.
* **Real-time Research & Synthesis:** Using Gemini's live connection to Google Search to compile current articles or check facts for debating classes.
* **Video Lesson Scaffolding:** Prompting Gemini to watch an educational video (e.g., from CrashCourse Literature) and generate active comprehension questions.

---

## Strengths & Weaknesses
### Strengths
* Deep integration with the Google ecosystem (Drive, Docs, Slides, Gmail).
* Natively multimodal: can analyze audio, images, and long video files in a single prompt.
* High-speed outputs and direct citation buttons linking back to Google search results.

### Weaknesses
* Text formatting inside the chat interface can sometimes require manual adjustment when copying to outside programs.
* Advanced features and large context limits require a Google One AI Premium subscription.

## Pricing & Free Plan
* **Free Tier:** Standard Gemini model with access to Workspace extensions and search capabilities.
* **Gemini Advanced ($20/month):** Access to Gemini 1.5 Pro, 2M token context window, and integration within Google Docs and Slides.
* **Google Workspace for Education Add-on:** Institutional licensing ensuring student data privacy and school-wide admin controls.

## Best Practices for Teachers
1. **Leverage Workspace Extensions:** Type `@Google Drive` or `@Google Docs` followed by your prompt to analyze files stored in your cloud storage.
2. **Use Video Inputs:** Paste a public YouTube video URL and ask Gemini to create a video-based guide or assessment.
3. **Double Check Information:** Click the "G" (double-check response) button at the bottom of the output to cross-verify the claims against active web pages.

## Example Prompts

### 1. YouTube Video Companion Guide
* **Purpose:** Create a listening and reading comprehension guide based on a YouTube video.
* **Inputs:** YouTube video URL (e.g., an educational biography of George Orwell).
* **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Please watch this video: [Insert YouTube Link]. Create a student handout for a Grade 10 English class. Include:
  1. A 3-sentence summary of the video.
  2. 5 chronological comprehension questions (with a teacher answer key).
  3. A critical thinking discussion prompt about the theme of propaganda.
  ```
* **Expected Output:** A structured PDF-ready document outline with summaries, questions, and answers.
* **Customization Tips:** Direct Gemini to output the response in a tabular format for clean copying.
