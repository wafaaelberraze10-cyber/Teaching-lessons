## Year-by-Year Milestones

### Year 1: Foundations & Observations
* **Focus:** Build theoretical understanding of child development, SLA (Second Language Acquisition), and literary history.
* **Key Actions:**
  - Complete 20+ hours of classroom observations.
  - Establish a solid GPA in core literature and linguistics courses.
  - Familiarize yourself with basic ELA curriculum standards (CCSS, CEFR).

### Year 2: Methods & Micro-Teaching
* **Focus:** Transition from *what* to teach to *how* to teach.
* **Key Actions:**
  - Deliver at least 3 "micro-teaching" lessons to your peers.
  - Master lesson planning frameworks (Backward Design, 5E Model).
  - Begin exploring EdTech tools (Google Workspace, Canva, basic AI prompts).

### Year 3: Practicum & Digital Portfolios
* **Focus:** Practical classroom experience under a cooperating teacher.
* **Key Actions:**
  - Complete a part-time teaching practicum (co-teaching individual lessons).
  - Launch a digital teaching portfolio (featuring lesson plans, mock worksheets, and your teaching philosophy).
  - Obtain basic free EdTech certifications (Google Educator L1, Microsoft Educator).

### Year 4: Internship & Clinical Practice
* **Focus:** Full immersion. Taking over complete classroom responsibilities for a full semester.
* **Key Actions:**
  - Lead unit plans, grading cycles, and parent communications under supervision.
  - Record video clips of your teaching for self-reflection and credentials.
  - Write your final teaching philosophy statement.

## Securing Your First Job
1. **Resume Construction:** Focus on student teaching results (e.g., *"Designed and taught a 4-week unit on Hamlet for 60 Grade 10 students, achieving a 92% mastery rate on formative rubrics"*).
2. **Demo Lesson Prep:** Prepare a modular 45-minute lesson plan that can scale up or down easily depending on school needs.
3. **LinkedIn & Networking:** Attend regional education conferences, connect with local school district recruiters, and share your digital portfolio link.
