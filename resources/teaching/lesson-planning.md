## Backward Design Framework (Wiggins & McTighe)
Instead of starting with classroom activities, Backward Design begins with the end goals:

1. **Identify Desired Results:** What should students know and be able to do at the end of the lesson? (e.g., *Identify the theme of a text and support it with textual evidence.*)
2. **Determine Acceptable Evidence:** How will you know students have met these goals? (e.g., *Formative exit ticket scoring 3/4 on the rubric.*)
3. **Plan Learning Experiences & Instruction:** What activities and direct instruction will lead students to these goals?

### How AI Helps:
Ask the AI to generate measurable behavioral objectives based on national curriculum standards, and then prompt it to design assessments *before* generating classroom activities.

## Gagne's 9 Events of Instruction
A systematic model for structuring active lessons:

1. **Gain Attention:** Quick hook (story, image, paradox).
2. **Inform Learners of Objectives:** Display target skills.
3. **Stimulate Recall of Prior Learning:** Quick retrieval review.
4. **Present the Content:** Direct instruction, modeling.
5. **Provide Learning Guidance:** Guided practice, graphic organizers.
6. **Elicit Performance:** Independent practice, writing.
7. **Provide Feedback:** Instant feedback, peer review.
8. **Assess Performance:** Quiz, exit ticket.
9. **Enhance Retention & Transfer:** Homework, real-world connection.

## The 5E Instructional Model
An inquiry-based framework particularly effective for student-centered literature lessons:

* **Engage:** Hook students and activate prior knowledge.
* **Explore:** Hands-on exploration of a text or concept (e.g., highlighting mystery clues).
* **Explain:** Introduce formal terms and definitions.
* **Elaborate:** Apply the concept to a new context.
* **Evaluate:** Assess student understanding.

## AI Prompts for Lesson Planning

### 1. Gagne's 9 Events Lesson Plan Builder
* **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Act as a master curriculum designer. Build a 60-minute lesson plan for a Grade 10 English class using Gagne's 9 Events of Instruction.
  Topic: Identifying Irony (situational, verbal, dramatic) in literature.
  Focus Standards: CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RL.9-10.4
  Please structure the output with clear timestamps, teacher scripts for "Present the Content", and student instructions for "Elicit Performance".
  ```

## Lesson Planning Checklist

- [ ] Clear, student-friendly objectives displayed on the board.
- [ ] Direct instruction does not exceed 15-20 consecutive minutes.
- [ ] Active student checking-for-understanding occurs every 5-7 minutes.
- [ ] Handouts and materials are fully prepared (digital links verified).
- [ ] A concrete assessment (exit ticket) is planned for the last 5 minutes.
