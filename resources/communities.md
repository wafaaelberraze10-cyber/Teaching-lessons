## Online Forums & Discussion Groups

### Reddit Communities (Subreddits)
* **r/teachers:** General community for schoolteachers to share experiences and seek advice.
* **r/ELAteachers:** Specifically for English Language Arts teachers. Great for sharing book lists and lesson reviews.
* **r/TEFL & r/ESL_Teachers:** Forums dedicated to teaching English as a foreign or second language.

### Professional Chat Groups
* **Discord Teaching Servers:** Communities like *Teachers' Lounge* and *English Teacher Connect* offer real-time grading support and template swaps.
* **LinkedIn Groups:** *TESOL International Association* and *EdTech Innovators* networks.

## Professional Teacher Organizations

### 1. TESOL International Association
* **Who it's for:** ESL, EFL, and bilingual educators.
* **Benefits:** A massive global network, dedicated Special Interest Groups (such as CALL - Computer Assisted Language Learning), research grants, job boards, and academic journals.
* **Link:** [https://www.tesol.org](https://www.tesol.org)

### 2. IATEFL (International Association of Teachers of English as a Foreign Language)
* **Who it's for:** UK and globally based EFL teachers.
* **Benefits:** UK-based with massive global reach, hosting 16 distinct Special Interest Groups (SIGs), monthly professional webinars, and practitioner publications.
* **Link:** [https://www.iatefl.org](https://www.iatefl.org)

### 3. NCTE (National Council of Teachers of English)
* **Who it's for:** ELA teachers and professors.
* **Benefits:** Publications, conferences, curriculum policy advocacy.
* **Link:** [https://ncte.org](https://ncte.org)

## AI Education Communities
* **AI in Education (Facebook/LinkedIn Groups):** Active teacher networks sharing daily prompt designs.
* **Edutopia AI Forums:** Dedicated threads on classroom technology integration.
* **OpenAI Developer Forum (Education Section):** Technical discussions on building custom GPT chatbots for schools.
