## Core Standards Frameworks

### Common Core State Standards (CCSS)
Used extensively across the US, separating ELA into:
* **Reading Literature (RL) & Informational Text (RI)**
* **Writing (W)**
* **Speaking & Listening (SL)**
* **Language (L)**
* *Example Standard:* `CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RL.9-10.1` refers to citing strong textual evidence to support analysis of what the text says explicitly.

### Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR)
The global gold standard for grading language proficiency (ESL/EFL):
* **A1 & A2 (Basic User):** Simple communication, high-frequency phrases.
* **B1 & B2 (Independent User):** Can describe experiences, explain opinions, read standard prose.
* **C1 & C2 (Proficient User):** Can understand implicit meaning, draft complex academic articles.

### Cambridge Assessment Standards
Aligns curriculum with international exams (KET, PET, FCE, CAE, CPE, and IGCSE ELA). Focuses on practical language acquisition, grammatical structures, and textual transformations.

## Vertical and Horizontal Alignment

```
Vertical Alignment:   Grade 9 ───► Grade 10 ───► Grade 11 (Progressive Complexity)
                                       ▲
Horizontal Alignment:                Week 1 ───► Week 2 ───► Week 3 (Logical Flow)
```

* **Vertical Alignment:** Ensuring that Grade 9 writing foundations (e.g., standard paragraphs) prepare students for Grade 10 essay expectations (e.g., comparative structures).
* **Horizontal Alignment:** Pacing lessons within a semester so that reading strategies (e.g., identifying theme) occur before writing assessments (e.g., literary thesis statements).

## AI-Assisted Standards Mapping

### 1. Cross-Mapping Standards Prompt (CCSS to CEFR)
* **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Act as an international school curriculum designer. Cross-map the Common Core ELA standard 'CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RI.9-10.2' (determining central ideas and analyzing development) to equivalent CEFR reading benchmarks. Explain what scaffolding a CEFR B1 level student requires to access this standard, and provide a list of three differentiation strategies.
  ```
