## Core ESL/EFL Professional Certifications

### 1. Cambridge CELTA (Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)
* **What it is:** The global gold standard entry-level qualification for teaching English as a foreign language.
* **Cost:** ~$1,500–$2,200 (depending on center).
* **Duration:** 4 weeks (intensive full-time) or 10–12 weeks (part-time/online).
* **Career Impact:** Essential for securing reputable jobs in universities, international schools, and top language institutes globally.
* **Link:** [Cambridge English - CELTA](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/find-a-centre/find-a-celta-centre/)

### 2. Trinity CertTESOL (Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)
* **What it is:** A direct competitor to the CELTA, accredited by Trinity College London and recognized worldwide.
* **Cost:** ~$1,400–$2,000.
* **Duration:** 4 weeks (full-time) or part-time.
* **Career Impact:** High. Matches CELTA recognition for international schools.

## Advanced Language Pedagogy Credentials

### 1. Cambridge DELTA (Diploma in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)
* **What it is:** An advanced qualification for experienced teachers covering deep theory, syllabus design, and training.
* **Cost:** ~$3,000–$4,500 (covers three separate modules).
* **Duration:** 9 months to 2 years (typically completed while working).
* **Career Impact:** Opens paths to Director of Studies, Teacher Trainer, and Curriculum Consultant roles.
* **Link:** [Cambridge English - DELTA](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/qualifications-and-tests/delta/)

## Academic & Post-Graduate Qualifications

### 1. PGCE (Postgraduate Certificate in Education) / State Teacher License
* **What it is:** The official university credential certifying classroom practice in the UK, Commonwealth countries, and international schools.
* **Cost:** ~$6,000–$15,000 (depending on residency and university).
* **Duration:** 1 year (full-time) including classroom teaching placements.
* **Career Impact:** Absolute requirement for public state schools and tier-1 international schools.

## Paid EdTech & Coaching Certifications

### 1. Google Certified Coach
* **What it is:** Validates your ability to coach other teachers in integrating Google tools to improve student outcomes.
* **Cost:** Free training; portfolio assessment and mentoring can require paid consultants depending on school support networks.
* **Duration:** ~20–30 hours of portfolio building.
* **Career Impact:** Prepares teachers for EdTech Coach, Coordinator, or Integrator roles.
