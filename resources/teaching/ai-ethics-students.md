## The ELA Rules of AI Engagement

Teach students the **"Three C's"** of responsible AI utilization:

* **1. Conceptualize, Don't Copy:** Use AI to brainstorm essay topics, outline paragraphs, or explain challenging vocabulary. Never copy-paste paragraphs directly from AI into an assignment.
* **2. Criticize & Verify:** Treat AI as an unreliable source. Students must verify every historical claim, quote, or statistical assertion against independent secondary sources.
* **3. Cite Transparently:** If AI assisted in structuring or refining a draft, the student must document their prompts and state which tools were used.

## Citing AI in Student Writing

Students must learn to credit AI models when they use them for research or outline development:

### MLA 9th Edition Standard
* **Format:** "Title of prompt" prompt. *Name of AI Tool*, version, Publisher (developer), Date of output, URL of tool.
* **Example:** "Analyze motifs of greed in Macbeth" prompt. *ChatGPT*, 4o version, OpenAI, 15 Mar. 2026, chatgpt.com.

### APA 7th Edition Standard
* **Format:** Developer. (Year). *AI Tool Name* (Version) [Large language model]. URL
* **Example:** OpenAI. (2026). *ChatGPT* (GPT-4o) [Large language model]. https://chatgpt.com

## Lesson Plan: The ELA Hallucination Hunt

* **Target Grade:** Grades 8–12 / CEFR B1–C2
* **Duration:** 45 Minutes
* **Objective:** Students will identify and edit false information generated by an AI chatbot.

### Steps:
1. **The Setup (10 min):** Project a prompt on the board: *"Write a 2-paragraph summary of the life of William Shakespeare, including details about his children, his lost years, and his friendship with Edgar Allan Poe."* (Note: Poe lived in a completely different century).
2. **Generate Output:** Generate the AI response. Have the students read it.
3. **The Hunt (20 min):** In pairs, students use textbooks or verified research databases to audit the AI's biography. They must find at least two historical "hallucinations" (errors) generated by the AI.
4. **Debrief (15 min):** Bring the class together. Discuss *why* the AI generated Poe as Shakespeare's friend and how search algorithms differ from human research.

## Student AI Honor Code Template

Copy and distribute this agreement at the start of writing units:

```text
I, [Student Name], agree to use generative AI tools responsibly. 
I promise that:
- Every sentence I submit in my final drafts represents my own voice and writing style.
- I will verify all facts, dates, and quotes generated by AI before including them.
- I will document my prompts in an appendix for any essay where AI assisted my planning.

Student Signature: _______________________ Date: _________
```
