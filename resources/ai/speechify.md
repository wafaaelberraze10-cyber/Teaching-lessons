## Overview
Speechify is a leading text-to-speech (TTS) accessibility application designed to assist readers of all ages. By transforming written text—including PDFs, web articles, physical handouts, and books—into natural-sounding audio with synchronized word highlighting, Speechify is an essential accommodation tool for students with learning differences like dyslexia or ADHD.

---

## Use Cases in the English Classroom
* **Reading Accommodations (IEP/504 Plans):** Supporting students with dyslexia, visual impairments, or reading challenges by allowing them to listen to texts as they read.
* **Student Proofreading Support:** Encouraging students to paste their drafts into Speechify. Listening to their own writing read aloud helps them identify grammar mistakes, run-on sentences, and awkward phrasing.
* **Vocabulary Acquisition:** Helping ESL/EFL students hear the correct pronunciation of unfamiliar words in real time while reading.
* **Audiobook Creation:** Converting digital worksheets or short stories into audio files for flipped classroom learning.

## Strengths & Weaknesses
### Strengths
* High-quality text tracking: highlights words as they are read, reinforcing sound-letter correspondence.
* Optical Character Recognition (OCR): allows students to scan physical pages with their phone camera and immediately listen to them.
* Wide range of premium natural voices and adjustable speeds (up to 4.5x).

### Weaknesses
* Free version has limited access to the highest-quality natural voices.
* Annual subscription model is relatively expensive for individual classrooms or student licenses.

## Pricing & Free Plan
* **Free Tier:** Basic text-to-speech, standard voices, adjustable speed.
* **Speechify Premium ($139/year):** Access to high-quality natural voices, OCR scanning, file uploads, and note-taking.
* **School/District Licensing:** Volume discounts for schools setting up campus-wide student accessibility accounts.

## Best Practices for Teachers
1. **Model the Proofreading Workflow:** Show students how listening to their own essays helps catch errors. If they hear a robotic voice stumble on a sentence, it usually means their sentence structure needs work.
2. **Synchronize Reading Speed:** Guide students to set the audio speed slightly faster than their natural reading speed to help build reading stamina, or slower to improve vocabulary tracking.
3. **Use the Browser Extension:** Install the Speechify Chrome extension on school Chromebooks to let students read online articles, quizzes, or research papers with audio support.

## Example Workflows

### 1. The Audio-Visual Reading Loop
* **Goal:** Improve vocabulary retention and reading engagement.
* **Setup:** Load a PDF of a classroom text into Speechify.
* **Instructions for Students:**
  1. Open the assignment in Speechify.
  2. Put on headphones and set the reading speed to a comfortable pace (e.g., 1.2x).
  3. Keep your eyes on the screen, following the highlighted words as the narrator reads.
  4. Pause and use Speechify's dictionary search when encountering an unfamiliar word.

## Classroom Examples
* **Listening Station:** Set up an iPad in the classroom library with the Speechify app loaded. Students who struggle with independent reading can take physical books, photograph the pages with the app, and listen to the text through headphones, helping them participate in class literature circles.
