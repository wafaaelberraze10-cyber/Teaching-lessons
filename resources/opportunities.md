## Classroom Project Funding (Crowdfunding)

### DonorsChoose
* **What it is:** Crowdfunding platform where public school teachers post projects (e.g., *"Graphic novels for Grade 9 ELA"*), and individuals or corporations fund them.
* **Best for:** Books, classroom furniture, and student technology.
* **Link:** [https://www.donorschoose.org](https://www.donorschoose.org)

## Academic Grants & Fellowships

### 1. Fulbright Distinguished Awards in Teaching Research Program
* **What it is:** Sponsored by the US State Department, allowing teachers to travel abroad for a semester to conduct educational research.
* **Link:** [Fulbright Teacher Exchanges](https://www.fulbrightteacherexchanges.org)

### 2. NEA Foundation Learning & Leadership Grants
* **What it is:** Provides $2,000 to $5,000 for educators to participate in professional development, conferences, or study groups.
* **Link:** [NEA Foundation](https://www.neafoundation.org)

## International & Postgraduate Scholarships

### 1. Fulbright FLTA (Foreign Language Teaching Assistant) Program
* **Type:** International Fellowship.
* **Benefit:** Fully Funded (Flights, monthly stipend, health insurance).
* **Target Audience:** Early-career English teachers.
* **Description:** A prestigious 9-month non-degree program funded by the U.S. Department of State to teach native languages and study ELA pedagogy.
* **Link:** [Fulbright FLTA Portal](https://foreign.fulbrightprogram.org)

### 2. British Council ELT Scholarships
* **Type:** Academic Grant.
* **Benefit:** Fully Funded (Tuition fees, residential visit, IELTS examination fee).
* **Target Audience:** English teachers in developing nations (e.g., India).
* **Description:** Funding for completing online MA or MSc in TESOL programs at participating UK universities.
* **Link:** [British Council India / Global](https://www.britishcouncil.org)

### 3. K.C. Mahindra Scholarships for Postgraduate Studies Abroad
* **Type:** Educational Trust Grant.
* **Benefit:** Variable Financial Grants (interest-free loans/scholarships).
* **Target Audience:** Graduate and postgraduate students seeking international exposure or diploma training.
* **Description:** Grants and scholarships for postgraduate studies abroad and diploma courses within India.
* **Link:** [Mahindra Education Trust](https://www.kcmet.org)

## Student Teacher Financial Aid

### 1. UP Scholarship (Post-Matric)
* **Type:** State Financial Aid.
* **Benefit:** Fee Reimbursement & Maintenance Allowance.
* **Target Audience:** B.Ed and M.Ed students in Uttar Pradesh, India from economically weaker backgrounds.
* **Description:** Financial assistance provided by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to pursue teaching degrees.
* **Link:** [UP Scholarship Portal](https://scholarship.up.gov.in)

### 2. TEACH Grant Program (US)
* **Type:** Federal Grant.
* **Benefit:** Up to $4,000/year.
* **Target Audience:** Students in the US who agree to teach in high-need fields at low-income schools for 4 years.
