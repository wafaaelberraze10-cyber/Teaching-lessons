## Classroom Project Funding (Crowdfunding)

### DonorsChoose
* **What it is:** Crowdfunding platform where public school teachers post projects (e.g., *"Graphic novels for Grade 9 ELA"*), and individuals or corporations fund them.
* **Best for:** Books, classroom furniture, and student technology.
* **Link:** [https://www.donorschoose.org](https://www.donorschoose.org)

## Academic Grants & Fellowships

### 1. Fulbright Distinguished Awards in Teaching Research Program
* **What it is:** Sponsored by the US State Department, allowing teachers to travel abroad for a semester to conduct educational research.
* **Link:** [Fulbright Teacher Exchanges](https://www.fulbrightteacherexchanges.org)

### 2. NEA Foundation Learning & Leadership Grants
* **What it is:** Provides $2,000 to $5,000 for educators to participate in professional development, conferences, or study groups.
* **Link:** [NEA Foundation](https://www.neafoundation.org)

## Student Teacher Scholarships
* **TEACH Grant Program (US):** Provides up to $4,000/year to students who agree to teach in high-need fields at low-income schools.
* **TESOL Travel Grants:** Funding for student members to attend the annual international convention.
