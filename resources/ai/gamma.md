## Overview
Gamma App is an AI-powered presentation and document generator. It transforms simple text outlines or prompts into visually stunning, interactive slide decks, document pages, or simple websites in seconds, saving teachers hours of manual design time.

---

## Use Cases in the English Classroom
* **Lecture Presentation Slides:** Creating slide decks on grammar concepts, literary movements, or rhetorical devices.
* **Interactive Study Handouts:** Designing clean, visually balanced student booklets or syllabus overviews.
* **Class Websites:** Instantly generating static web portals for specific projects or student resource centers.
* **Syllabus Design:** Packaging course information into an elegant, scannable visual layout.

---

## Strengths & Weaknesses
### Strengths
* High-speed design: generates full slides complete with layouts, headings, and images in under a minute.
* Responsive: designs auto-fit desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
* Export capabilities: exports directly to PDF or Microsoft PowerPoint formats.

### Weaknesses
* Free plan has limited credits (refreshed through referral links).
* AI-selected imagery can sometimes feel generic (requires manual refinement).
* Text sizing and custom layouts have less manual editing flexibility than standard Google Slides or PowerPoint.

## Best Practices for Teachers
1. **Provide Structured Outlines:** Do not rely on short prompts. Copy-paste a detailed Markdown outline (created by ChatGPT or Claude) to ensure the slides contain accurate lesson content.
2. **Use Cards for Activities:** Utilize Gamma's "Card" blocks to split class lessons into distinct steps (e.g., Warm-up, Guided Work, Discussion, Exit Ticket).
3. **Embed Rich Media:** Insert YouTube videos, Miro boards, or Google Forms directly into the slides to make them interactive.

## Example Prompts

### 1. Introduction to Romanticism Slide Deck Outline
* **Purpose:** Generate a visual presentation introduction to the Romantic era.
* **Inputs:** Target Grade Level (High School), Outline of Romantic poetry.
* **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Create an 8-card presentation for a High School English class introducing Romanticism in literature.
  Cards outline:
  1. Title: The Romantic Era (1798–1837)
  2. Historical Context (Industrial Revolution backlash)
  3. Key Themes (Nature, Emotion, Individualism)
  4. William Wordsworth & Nature
  5. Samuel Taylor Coleridge & The Supernatural
  6. Mary Shelley & Gothic Romanticism
  7. Comparative Table: Enlightenment vs. Romanticism
  8. Exit Ticket Activity
  Use a warm, classic bookish theme.
  ```
* **Expected Output:** An 8-slide interactive presentation with matching theme, card designs, and placeholder images.
* **Customization Tips:** Swap images manually with authentic historical portraits.
