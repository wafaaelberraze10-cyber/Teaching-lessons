## Year-by-Year Milestones

### Year 1: Foundations & Observations
* **Academic Focus:** Psychology of learning, structural fundamentals of English grammar, and baseline digital literacy (Google/Microsoft Workspace tools).
* **Key Actions:**
  - Complete 20+ hours of classroom observations.
  - Establish a solid GPA in core literature and linguistics courses.
  - Familiarize yourself with basic ELA curriculum standards (CCSS, CEFR).

### Year 2: Methods & Micro-Teaching
* **Academic Focus:** Instructional design, methodology, Second Language Acquisition (SLA) theories, and evaluating digital education tools.
* **Key Actions:**
  - Deliver at least 3 "micro-teaching" lessons to your peers.
  - Master lesson planning frameworks (Backward Design, 5E Model).
  - Begin exploring EdTech tools (Google Workspace, Canva, basic AI prompts).

### Year 3: Practicum & Digital Portfolios
* **Academic Focus:** Advanced assessment literacy, integration of AI, Computational Thinking in Computer-Assisted Language Learning (CTCALL), and prompt engineering.
* **Key Actions:**
  - Complete a part-time teaching practicum (co-teaching individual lessons).
  - Launch a digital teaching portfolio (featuring lesson plans, mock worksheets, and your teaching philosophy).
  - Obtain basic free EdTech certifications (Google Educator L1, Microsoft Educator).

### Year 4 & Internship
* **Academic Focus:** Micro-teaching, full classroom takeovers, and the **"Cognitive Mirror" paradigm** (testing explanations on AI by prompting the chatbot to act as a teachable student to trigger the Protégé Effect).
* **Key Actions:**
  - Lead unit plans, grading cycles, and parent communications under supervision during clinical practice.
  - Record video clips of your teaching for self-reflection and credentials.
  - Build a comprehensive digital portfolio showcasing micro-teaching, curriculum designs, and custom AI prompts.

### Graduation & Career Growth
* **Academic Focus:** Systematization, Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) systems, action research, and advanced credentials (e.g., M.Ed or DELTA).
* **Key Actions:**
  - Secure state licensing and begin application processes for international markets.
  - Present action research at regional practitioner conferences.
