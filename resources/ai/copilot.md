## Overview
Microsoft Copilot (formerly Bing Chat) is Microsoft's primary generative AI assistant. Natively integrated into the Microsoft 365 suite (Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams) and the Microsoft Edge browser, it is a robust tool for educators in schools that leverage Microsoft technology.

---

## Use Cases in the English Classroom
* **Handout Creation in Word:** Drafting classroom worksheets, reading check questions, and guides directly within Microsoft Word files.
* **Interactive Presentations:** Exporting structured curriculum outlines from Copilot into PowerPoint to automatically generate slide decks.
* **Inline Reading Summaries:** Using the Edge browser Copilot sidebar to summarize long PDF studies or news articles without leaving the page.
* **Professional Communications:** Generating administrative emails, department meeting minutes, and newsletters in Outlook.

---

## Strengths & Weaknesses
### Strengths
* Seamless integration with Microsoft Office 365 programs.
* Commercial Data Protection: When signed in with school credentials, prompts and user data are not saved or used to train public models.
* Real-time internet access via Bing Search, with footnotes linking directly to sources.

### Weaknesses
* Microsoft Office integration requires paid institutional subscriptions or Copilot Pro licenses.
* Edge browser sidebar interface can feel distracting or cluttered to beginners.

---

## Pricing & Free Plan
* **Free Tier:** Access to web search and basic chat capabilities at [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com) and in Microsoft Edge.
* **Copilot Pro ($20/month):** Integration into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook for personal accounts.
* **Copilot for Microsoft 365 Education:** Institutional pricing for schools, which includes advanced security controls and campus-wide integrations.

## Best Practices for Teachers
1. **Ensure Safe Sign-In:** Always sign in with your school Microsoft account. Look for the "Protected" shield badge to verify that your student-related queries comply with school privacy rules.
2. **Use Edge Sidebar:** Open a long academic PDF in Edge, click the Copilot icon in the top right, and type: *"Create a 5-question multiple-choice quiz based on this document."*
3. **Toggle Chat Styles:** Choose "Creative" mode for writing prompts and activities, or "Precise" mode for curriculum timelines and syllabus details.

## Example Prompts

### 1. Microsoft Word Lesson Plan Builder
* **Purpose:** Generate a complete lesson plan formatted for direct export to Microsoft Word.
* **Inputs:** Subject (e.g., Active vs. Passive Voice), Grade Level (e.g., Grade 7).
* **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Act as a middle school English teacher. Create a 45-minute lesson plan on distinguishing between 'Active' and 'Passive' voice. Include a warm-up activity, a direct instruction script, a guided practice exercise with 10 sentences, and an exit ticket. Format this using clean headers so I can export it directly into a Word document.
  ```
* **Expected Output:** A fully structured, ready-to-copy lesson plan sheet.
* **Customization Tips:** Instruct Copilot to add an "Answer Key" section at the very end.

## Classroom Examples
* **Slide Generation:** Ask Copilot to *"Create an outline for a presentation on Shakespeare's Globe Theatre"*. Open PowerPoint, select "Export" or use the Copilot icon inside PowerPoint to transform that outline into a themed slide deck with visual elements.
