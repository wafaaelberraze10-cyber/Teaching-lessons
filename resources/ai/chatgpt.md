## Overview
ChatGPT, developed by OpenAI, is a conversational large language model based on the GPT architecture. It is an invaluable partner for English, ESL, and EFL teachers, serving as a co-planner, feedback generator, and content creation assistant.

---

## Use Cases in the English Classroom
* **Differentiated Reading Material:** Adjusting the lexile level or vocabulary complexity of a text for different student levels.
* **Grammar & Sentence Analysis:** Generating contextual grammar exercises or explaining complex syntactic structures.
* **Writing Feedback & Scaffolding:** Creating rubrics, outlining essay drafts, or providing initial proofreading support.
* **Interactive Dialogue Partners:** Enabling ESL/EFL students to practice speaking and writing in simulated real-world scenarios.

---

## Strengths & Weaknesses
### Strengths
* High versatility across general pedagogical planning and creative writing.
* Exceptional understanding of grammar, spelling, and sentence structures.
* Highly customisable through detailed prompts and system instructions (Custom Instructions / GPTs).

### Weaknesses
* Can produce "hallucinations" (confident but incorrect facts or historical details).
* Default output styling can sound formulaic or overly verbose (requires prompt tuning).
* Free tier limits access to the highest-performing model (GPT-4o) during peak hours.

## Best Practices for Teachers
1. **Assign a Role:** Always begin prompts by defining ChatGPT's role (e.g., *"Act as a Cambridge CELTA certified English teacher"*).
2. **Provide Constraints:** Explicitly state what to avoid (e.g., *"Do not use passive voice in the outputs"*, *"Keep sentences under 15 words"*).
3. **Include Context:** Supply age, English proficiency level (CEFR: A1-C2), and class size.
4. **Use Few-Shot Examples:** Give ChatGPT 1 or 2 examples of the exact style and format you want.

## Example Prompts

### 1. Differentiated Text Simplifier
* **Purpose:** Convert a complex text into a simplified version for lower-level learners.
* **Inputs:** 
  * Target Level: `CEFR B1`
  * Text: `Insert Shakespearean passage or modern editorial`
* **Prompt:**
  ```text
  Act as an expert ESL curriculum designer. Simplify the following text to a CEFR B1 level. Do not lose the core message, but replace complex idioms, passive verbs, and obscure vocabulary with high-frequency B1 vocabulary. Provide a list of 5 key words simplified, with definitions in context.
  [Insert Text Here]
  ```
* **Expected Output:** A simplified, readable paragraph followed by a short glossary of vocabulary words.
* **Customization Tips:** Change the target level to `Grade 6 Lexile 800L` for middle school native speakers.

## Classroom Examples
* **Peer Editing Companion:** Project ChatGPT on the smartboard. Input a paragraph with intentional errors. Have students find the errors, and then ask ChatGPT to explain *why* those are grammatical errors, comparing student answers to ChatGPT's analysis.
